# About this collection 

AI is becoming a bigger part of the education system, more specifically college education, but it can be difficult to know which AI tools and information are actually useful and benefit you. This collection was created to help college students understand practical ways they can use AI for school, productivity, safety, and career preparation while also understanding the risks  that come with it. Instead of trying to cover every possible use of AI, this collection focuses specifically on how students can use AI as a tool without allowing it to replace their own thinking and decision-making.

I split this collection into three different categories: School & Learning, Career Preparation, and AI Safety. The School & Learning category focuses more on resources about studying, research, writing, and responsible use of AI in education. The Career Preparation category includes resources about resumes, interviews, careers, and the changing workplace. The AI Safety category covers topics such as privacy, misinformation, deepfakes, and scams.

The collection includes different types of sources, including articles, research, organizations, videos, podcasts, TED Talks, and other resources. Each source includes an annotation explaining what it covers, why it is useful, who would benefit from it, and any limitations it may have. The goal is to give students a collection they actually can use instead of simply giving them a list.
